Heals back <red>25%</red> of dealt damage to others. This effect cannot be stacked.

Netherite citrine ring:

Netherite jade ring:
Grants <blue>20%</blue> bonus movement speed and jump boost. This effect additionally works while riding (on tamed mobs or vehicles).

Netherite tanzanite ring:

Netherite sapphire ring:
Grants <blue>50%</blue> bonus movement speed in water or lava, <blue>50%</blue> more breath and reduces burn time by <blue>50%</blue>.

Topaz ring:
Grants <blue>+1</blue> luck.

Netherite topaz ring:
Grants <blue>+2</blue> luck and provides bonus chance for:
- Rolling bonus rare loot inside chest. (10%)
- Applying looting twice on mob kill (10%)

- Mining 2-8 gold nuggets out of quartz ore. (10%)
- Mining diamonds out of lapis or emerald ores. (5%)
